# PRODUCT

## Product
The public website of Écoles Belges au Maroc (EBM): two Belgian schools in Morocco
(Casablanca · Dar Bouazza, and Rabat · Hay Riad) teaching the programme of the Fédération
Wallonie-Bruxelles from the classe d'accueil (2 years old) to the CESS (18). About 2 000 pupils,
118 teachers all trained and qualified in Belgium, network founded in 2014, an outdoor school
(école du dehors) on 1.5 hectares at Casablanca.

## Audience
1. Prospective families (primary): mostly on phones, comparing schools, need pedagogy, campus,
   fees and admission steps, then want to book a visit or apply.
2. Current families: dates, school life, practical info, Espace Eduka.
3. Alumni and secondary students: pathways, events, staying connected.
4. EBM staff: edit content without developers (Payload CMS).
5. Partners and future staff: network, values, contacts.

## Job of the site
Make EBM's quality legible in seconds, then put two actions within one tap everywhere:
« Visiter l'école » and « Candidater ».

## Surface modes
Home and campus pages: Persuade. Admissions, fees, calendar, forms: Operate. FAQ and
pedagogy: Read.

## Brand commitments (pinned)
- Typefaces: Playfair Display (titles) + Avenir Next (text), from EBM's brand guide.
- Palette: terracotta, olive, sand, cream from EBM's brand guide; logo colours only in the logo.
- Visual direction approved by Ayoub: the "version 2" canvas design (dark top bar, header with
  dropdown menus, full-bleed photo slider with overlapping highlights, collage, figures,
  Instagram, photo CTA band, light footer).
- Real photography of EBM campuses and students (parental authorisation required per photo).
- French at launch; English/Arabic (RTL) later.

## Constraints
Mobile-first, pages ready in ~2.5 s on 4G (target < 2 s), WCAG 2.1 AA, Moroccan law 09-08
for any personal data, admissions records stay in Eduka.
